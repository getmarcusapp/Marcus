Stoic Quotes, With Sources

Every passage below is cited to a book and a chapter, because most of what circulates as Stoic wisdom is not, and a good deal of it is not Stoic. These are drawn from the same checked corpus the app reads from.

We audited our own quote library one passage at a time and removed the ones that were not what they claimed to be. The findings are on our list of misattributed Stoic quotes.

  • Stoic Quotes on Death

    Twelve passages on mortality from Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus and Musonius Rufus, each with its source.

  • Stoic Quotes on Perception and Judgment

    Ten passages on the Stoic claim that events do not disturb us, our judgments about them do.

  • Stoic Quotes on Anxiety and Fear

    Seven passages on imagined trouble, from Seneca, Marcus Aurelius and Epictetus, each with its source.

  • Stoic Quotes on Discipline and Self-Mastery

    Twelve passages on habit, attention and the use of time, with chapter and verse.

  • Stoic Quotes on Character

    Ten passages on what a person is made of, and how the Stoics thought it was built.

  • Stoic Quotes on Dealing with Other People

    Nine passages on difficult people, insult, anger and what we owe each other.

Marcus turns these into a daily practice for iOS: a morning preparation, a daily reading matched to what you are working on, and an honest evening examination.
