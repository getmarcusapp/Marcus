Daily Meditations

One edition each morning: a verified passage from the Stoics, the moment in history that produced it, and an unforced line to the world as it is today. Two minutes. Free.

Sunday, July 26, 2026

On the spoken word

"If you didn't learn these things in order to demonstrate them in practice, what did you learn them for?" — Epictetus, Discourses

Saturday, July 25, 2026

On looking back

"Nowhere can man find a quieter or more untroubled retreat than in his own soul." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, IV.3

Friday, July 24, 2026

On what we borrow

"Never say of anything, 'I have lost it'; but, 'I have returned it.' Is your child dead? It is returned." — Epictetus, Enchiridion

Thursday, July 23, 2026

On what outlasts us

"Whatever you do, do it not for the sake of a reward, but for the thing itself." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book VII

Wednesday, July 22, 2026

On beginning again

Confine yourself to the present. — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VIII.7

Tuesday, July 21, 2026

On our own work

"In the morning when thou risest unwillingly, let this thought be present—I am rising to the work of a human being." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book 5

Monday, July 20, 2026

On winning and losing

"It is difficult to combine and unite these two qualities, the carefulness of one who is affected by circumstances, and the intrepidity of one who heeds them not." — Epictetus, Discourses, Book II

Sunday, July 19, 2026

On what we overlook

"Nowhere can man find a quieter or more untroubled retreat than in his own soul, above all when he has within him such thoughts that by looking into them he is immediately at perfect ease." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book IV

Saturday, July 18, 2026

On what remains

"Observe constantly that all things take place by change, and accustom thyself to consider that the nature of the Universe loves nothing so much as to change the things which are and to make new things like them." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book IV

Friday, July 17, 2026

On the unknown

"Nature has given us the seeds of knowledge, not knowledge itself." — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius (Letter CXX)

Thursday, July 16, 2026

On perspective

"Constantly regard the universe as one living being, having one substance and one soul." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book IV

Wednesday, July 15, 2026

On death

You could leave life right now. Let that determine what you do and say and think. — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations II.11

Tuesday, July 14, 2026

On enough

Wealth consists not in having great possessions, but in having few wants. — Attributed to Epictetus

Monday, July 13, 2026

On adversity

The greater the difficulty, the more glory in surmounting it. Skillful pilots gain their reputation from storms and tempests. — Epictetus, as recorded in the Discourses

Sunday, July 12, 2026

On time

It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it. — Seneca, On the Shortness of Life

Saturday, July 11, 2026

On other people

When you wake up in the morning, tell yourself: the people I deal with today will be meddling, ungrateful, arrogant, dishonest, jealous, and surly. — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations II.1

Friday, July 10, 2026

On the examined day

I will keep constant watch over myself and, most usefully, will put each day up for review. — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius I

Thursday, July 9, 2026

On what we control

Seek not that the things which happen should happen as you wish; but wish the things which happen to be as they are, and you will have a tranquil flow of life. — Epictetus, Enchiridion VIII

Wednesday, July 8, 2026

On beginning again

Confine yourself to the present. — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VIII.7
