One edition each morning: a verified passage from the Stoics, the moment in history that produced it, and an unforced line to the world as it is today. Two minutes. Free.

Friday, August 7, 2026

On the whole of a life

"As it is with a play, so it is with life: what matters is not how long the acting lasts, but how good it is." — Seneca, Letters from a Stoic, Letter 77

Thursday, August 6, 2026

On tending what remains

"That which has grown old is the very thing which time changes: nothing perishes wholly." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VIII.6

Wednesday, August 5, 2026

On single-mindedness

"To be everywhere is to be nowhere." — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius, Letter 2

Tuesday, August 4, 2026

On taking your share

"Remember that you must behave in life as at a dinner party. Is anything brought around to you? Put out your hand and take a moderate share. Does it pass by you? Do not stop it. Is it not yet come? Do not stretch your desire towards it, but wait till it reaches you." — Epictetus, Enchiridion 15

Monday, August 3, 2026

On what replaces us

"Some things are hurrying into existence, and others are hurrying out of it; and of that which is coming into existence part is already extinguished." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book VI

Sunday, August 2, 2026

On the verdict of others

"I have often wondered how it is that every man loves himself more than all the rest of men, but yet sets less value on his own opinion of himself than on the opinion of others." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book XII

Saturday, August 1, 2026

On transformation

"Loss is nothing else but change, and change is Nature's delight." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

Friday, July 31, 2026

On staying yourself

"Waste no more time arguing about what a good man should be. Be one." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations

Thursday, July 30, 2026

On inheriting a role

"We are not given a short life but we make it short, and we are not ill-supplied but wasteful of it." — Seneca, On the Shortness of Life

Wednesday, July 29, 2026

On new work

"As long as you live, keep learning how to live." — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius, Letter 76

Tuesday, July 28, 2026

On adversity

The greater the difficulty, the more glory in surmounting it. Skillful pilots gain their reputation from storms and tempests. — Epictetus, as recorded in the Discourses

Monday, July 27, 2026

On finishing

"Every day, therefore, should be regulated as if it were the one that brings up the rear, the one that rounds out and completes our life." — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius, XII

Sunday, July 26, 2026

On the spoken word

"If you didn't learn these things in order to demonstrate them in practice, what did you learn them for?" — Epictetus, Discourses

Saturday, July 25, 2026

On looking back

"Nowhere can man find a quieter or more untroubled retreat than in his own soul." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, IV.3

Friday, July 24, 2026

On what we borrow

"Never say of anything, 'I have lost it'; but, 'I have returned it.' Is your child dead? It is returned." — Epictetus, Enchiridion

Thursday, July 23, 2026

On what outlasts us

"Whatever you do, do it not for the sake of a reward, but for the thing itself." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book VII

Wednesday, July 22, 2026

On beginning again

Confine yourself to the present. — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations VIII.7

Tuesday, July 21, 2026

On our own work

"In the morning when thou risest unwillingly, let this thought be present—I am rising to the work of a human being." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book 5

Monday, July 20, 2026

On winning and losing

"It is difficult to combine and unite these two qualities, the carefulness of one who is affected by circumstances, and the intrepidity of one who heeds them not." — Epictetus, Discourses, Book II

Sunday, July 19, 2026

On what we overlook

"Nowhere can man find a quieter or more untroubled retreat than in his own soul, above all when he has within him such thoughts that by looking into them he is immediately at perfect ease." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book IV

Saturday, July 18, 2026

On what remains

"Observe constantly that all things take place by change, and accustom thyself to consider that the nature of the Universe loves nothing so much as to change the things which are and to make new things like them." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book IV

Friday, July 17, 2026

On the unknown

"Nature has given us the seeds of knowledge, not knowledge itself." — Seneca, Letters to Lucilius (Letter CXX)

Thursday, July 16, 2026

On perspective

"Constantly regard the universe as one living being, having one substance and one soul." — Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, Book IV

On death
