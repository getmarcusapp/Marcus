There is a line that runs through everything you meet. On one side, what is yours. On the other, what is not. Nearly all of your peace depends on knowing which is which.
[[16]]
Epictetus put it plainly: Some things are under our control, while others are not under our control.
[[6]]
Up to us: our judgments, our choices, what we intend, what we do. Not up to us: the outcome, the opinions of others, the body, the past, the world as it insists on being.
[[19]]
Bring to mind one thing that presses on you now. Not the largest thing. Just one. A conversation. A result you are waiting on. A person you cannot move.
[[31]]

[[16]]

Take the part that is not yours, and set it down. Not with resignation. With accuracy. It was never in your hands. Carrying it only spends the strength you will need.
